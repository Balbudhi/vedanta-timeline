# Ramakrishna, Sarada Devi, and Vivekananda: source inventory and quotation controls

This is an inventory, not an interpretation of the lineage. It records which
local witnesses can support a primary-source claim, which can only help locate
one, and what remains missing. A source is not treated as a quotation witness
merely because it is searchable.

## Ramakrishna

| Witness | Language and status | Proper use |
|---|---|---|
| *Śrī Śrī Rāmakṛṣṇa Kathāmṛta*, vol. 1, ninth-edition scan | Bengali; public page-image witness | The edition/page control for a Bengali quotation in vol. 1. It is not a full searchable edition. |
| *Kathāmṛta* portal capture | Bengali Unicode HTML; private, 523 captured pages | Search and comparison witness. The extracted UTF-8 text preserves visible Bengali Unicode, but every consequential quotation should retain its portal-page address and be checked against a printed/page-image witness where available. |
| Nikhilananda, *The Gospel of Sri Ramakrishna* | English HTML; private complete capture | Translation witness only. It may establish how Nikhilananda translates a passage; it cannot settle a disputed Bengali word or grammar. |

The portal capture records the familiar sentence **“যিনিই ব্রহ্ম তিনিই শক্তি …
দুটা আলাদা জিনিস নয়। একই জিনিস।”** Its grammar establishes emphatic
co-reference (the very one who is Brahman is Śakti) and denies two separate
things. It does not itself decide a later technical question about “mutual
ontological dependence.” That conclusion requires a separate argument.

## Sarada Devi

| Witness | Language and status | Proper use |
|---|---|---|
| *Śrī Śrī Māyer Kathā*, DLI/Internet Archive scan | Bengali; public page-image witness | Present citation and edition control. The Internet Archive OCR is useful for locating candidate pages, but contains errors and is not a quotation witness. |
| RKMVU *Mayer Katha* web transcription | Bengali; private discovery copy | **Do not quote.** It labels itself AI-assisted, and its extracted plaintext has encoding corruption. It remains only to find a possible passage for verification in the scan or a future verified edition. |

No verified clean Bengali transcription of Sarada Devi is currently committed.
That absence is recorded rather than covered by a silent machine-OCR
substitution. A Sarada quotation in a published analytical article must be
transcribed from the page image, cite its edition/page, and be independently
checked before use.

## Vivekananda

| Witness | Language and status | Proper use |
|---|---|---|
| *Complete Works*, vols. 1–9 | English; public, searchable local text captures | Full English working corpus. Each file names its portal volume source and retained page routes; cite the volume, work/lecture/letter, and local line/page route. |
| *Bāṇī o Racanā* | Bengali | Not yet held as a verified local edition. It may not be silently replaced with an English *Complete Works* passage when the claim concerns Bengali wording. |

## Required discipline

1. Give the original-language wording first whenever an argument depends on a
   word such as *vijñāna*, *abheda*, or *śakti*.
2. Identify the speaker, recorder/editor, work, date or section, edition, and
   page/portal route before drawing an inference.
3. Label a translation as a translation. Do not turn a translator's theological
   paraphrase into Bengali evidence.
4. Distinguish the plain semantic entailment of a sentence from a constructive
   Vedāntic interpretation built from it.

These controls are deliberately asymmetric: they permit a strong claim where
there is a controlled witness, and require a smaller claim where there is not.
