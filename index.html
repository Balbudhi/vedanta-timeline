About this site

This entire site is AI-generated. The thinker entries, work summaries, key-passage selections, any Sanskrit transcription and Pāṇinian breakdown, and the comparative-claim analyses were all composed by large language models, working from primary texts and from the standard scholarly editions cited in each entry. The site aims to offer a literal, minimally interpretive glimpse of the tradition: each thinker is presented on the thinker's own terms, in the grammar of the source texts, with verifiable citations.

The intent is to allow novel engagement with the tradition through citations to specific loci that an English-only reader can verify.

Every claim should be verifiable against the cited primary text.

Code, data, and editorial standards are at github.com/Balbudhi/vedanta-timeline. The site is intentionally non-indexed; share by direct link only.
