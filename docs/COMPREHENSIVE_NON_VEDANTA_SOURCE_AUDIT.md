# Comprehensive non-Vedānta source audit

Research audit, 2026-08-04. This is a curation and source-recovery document,
not a roster-import list. It records texts, schools, and source hubs that may
be needed to understand a thinker, but none of those is itself a timeline card.
The timeline is person-centered: a card must identify a named thinker or
teacher with a recoverable intellectual contribution. Its purpose is to keep
that standard from being weakened by confusing a surviving work, a translated
work, an opponent's report, a traditional attribution, and a modern
reconstruction.

## Inclusion rule

Add a person only when a real historical contribution can be stated and the
person's thought is recoverable from an authored text, secure fragments, or
substantial attributed material. A named teacher may be included when the
teacher—not an anonymous text or a school—is the historical intellectual unit.
Do not add schools, positions, anonymous texts, collective corpora, eponyms,
mythic founders, or bare names in hostile catalogues as thinker cards. Keep
those only as source context for qualified people.

### Survival labels

Use these visible labels on every non-standard entry.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Indic primary text survives` | An authored or anonymous Indic-language text can be read directly. |
| `Translation survives` | The Indic original is lost or incomplete but a Chinese/Tibetan/etc. witness preserves a substantial text. |
| `Fragments / cited voice` | A named view is recoverable from quotations or substantial reports. |
| `Opponent reconstruction` | No own text; doctrine is reported by rivals and must be stated as such. |
| `Attribution complex` | Text survives, but its author, date, or historical identity remains uncertain. |

## Research candidates and source map

### Lost and early Śramaṇa traditions

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Person | **Udbhaṭa / Udbhaṭabhaṭṭa** | A distinctive Cārvāka revision of *pramāṇa*, *prameya*, and body-consciousness questions. | *Tattvavṛtti* fragments preserved by later writers. | `Fragments / cited voice`; added with an explicit source caveat, while a fragment-level bibliography remains a source-acquisition task. |

**Do not add:** Bṛhaspati as a historical biographical author, or Bhāvivikta as
a thinker: the first is a traditional ascription for the lost *Bārhaspatya-
Sūtra* and the second has no recoverable doctrine. Keep both in the Cārvāka
source dossier. Jayarāśi is already the exceptional direct-text case and should
remain carefully labelled Cārvāka-adjacent rather than a simple spokesman for
all Lokāyata.

## Essential founder and source-hub correction

The current roster contains neither a Buddha nor Mahāvīra record. For a
comprehensive Indian-philosophy timeline, that is a larger distortion than many
smaller author gaps.

- Add **Gautama Buddha / Śākyamuni** as a `canonical_teacher` with a
  multi-recensional transmitted corpus: Pāli Nikāyas, Chinese Āgamas, and
  Sanskrit/Tibetan parallels. Do not claim autograph authorship.
- Add **Mahāvīra / Vardhamāna (Nigaṇṭha Nātaputta)** as a `canonical_teacher`
  with Śvetāmbara Āgamas, later *Kalpa-sūtra* biography, and Digambara
  counter-traditions. Do not collapse this evidence into an authored book.

Their dating should be conventionally fifth century BCE with a visible
chronology-disputed note. DN 2 is useful as a source for their shared early
intellectual landscape, not as neutral biography.

## Already present or already acquired: do not duplicate

The live roster and corpus have recently gained several draft records, including
Māṇikyanandi, Prabhācandra, Vādideva, Vasumitra, Yaśomitra, Manorathanandin,
Prajñākaragupta, Aruḷnandi, Umāpati, Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha, Puṇyānanda, Śivānanda
Yogin, Śrīpati, Śrīkumāra, Trilocanaśiva, Bhagavadutpala, and Rājānaka Rāma.
Do not re-add them.

The repository also already holds primary material that needs linking before
new acquisition is prioritized: Meykaṇṭār, Aruḷnandi, Umapati, Bhartṛhari,
Pāṇini, Kātyāyana/Patañjali grammar, Gaṅgeśa, Praśastapāda, Samantabhadra,
Siddhasena, Māṇikyanandi, and a substantial Kashmir Śaiva corpus. These are
provenance/visibility problems, not source absences.

## Data-model changes required before bulk expansion

1. Add `entity_kind`: `historical_author`, `canonical_teacher`,
   `school_or_community`, `reconstructed_position`, `anonymous_text`, or
   `attribution_complex_text`.
2. Add `evidence_basis`, `own_corpus_survival`, `reconstruction_confidence`,
   and per-claim source provenance. This prevents an opponent's report from
   rendering as if it were an authorial quotation.
3. Link every local source through `primary_corpus_paths` and distinguish
   `clean-on-disk`, `scan-on-disk`, `partial-on-disk`, and
   `translation-on-disk`. The current source status is stale: many source files
   are present but not connected to their thinker records.
4. Register the existing `vyakarana-comparator` and `alankara-comparator`
   lanes in `data/registries/schools.json`. Without this, serious existing
   grammar and aesthetics thinkers remain visually obscured.
5. Give Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta a proper lane rather than coloring it as Kashmir
   Śaiva Siddhānta.
